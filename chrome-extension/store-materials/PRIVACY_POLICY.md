# Privacy Policy for fscrape Chrome Extension

**Last Updated:** October 4, 2025

## Overview

fscrape is a Chrome extension that helps users track and analyze Reddit posts. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the extension works.

## Data Collection

### What Data is Collected

When you use fscrape, the following data is collected **locally in your browser**:

- **Reddit Post Data:** Post titles, authors, URLs, scores, comment counts, timestamps, flairs, and post metadata (NSFW status, locked status, stickied status)
- **Subreddit Tracking:** List of subreddits you have pinned for tracking
- **Onboarding State:** Whether you have seen the welcome modal and pin button tutorial
- **Timestamps:** When posts were scraped from Reddit

### How Data is Collected

Data is collected when:
1. You pin a subreddit for tracking by clicking the pin button
2. You scroll through Reddit pages while a subreddit is pinned
3. The extension automatically detects and saves visible Reddit posts

## Data Storage

**All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage APIs:**
- Chrome Local Storage (for settings and onboarding state)
- IndexedDB (for Reddit posts and subreddit data)

**Important:**
- No data is sent to external servers
- No data is uploaded to the cloud
- No data is shared with third parties
- All data remains on your device

## Data Usage

The collected data is used exclusively for:
- Displaying statistics in the extension popup
- Generating analytics in the dashboard (heatmaps, trends, optimal posting times)
- Exporting data in JSON or CSV format when you explicitly request it

## Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

- **View Your Data:** Access all tracked posts through the dashboard
- **Export Your Data:** Download all data as JSON or CSV at any time
- **Delete Your Data:** Use the "Clear All Data" button to permanently delete all tracked data
- **Unpin Subreddits:** Stop tracking specific subreddits at any time

## Third-Party Services

This extension does **not** use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## Reddit API

This extension does **not** use the Reddit API. It works by reading publicly visible content from Reddit web pages as you browse them, similar to how you would manually copy information.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions:

- **Storage:** To save tracked posts and settings locally in your browser
- **Active Tab:** To read Reddit post data from the current page
- **Side Panel:** To display the analytics dashboard
- **Host Permissions (reddit.com):** To run on Reddit pages and collect post data

## Data Retention

Data is retained indefinitely in your browser's local storage until you:
- Manually delete it using the "Clear All Data" button
- Uninstall the extension (which clears all extension data)
- Clear your browser's site data

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will indicate when changes were made. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally in your browser:
- Data security depends on your browser's security and your device's security
- We recommend using strong passwords and keeping your browser updated
- If you share your device with others, they may be able to access the extension's data

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the extension:
- Open an issue on our GitHub repository
- Contact us at the email listed in the Chrome Web Store

## Compliance

This extension complies with:
- Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
- Google API Services User Data Policy
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) principles

## Your Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:
- **Access:** View all data collected (via the dashboard)
- **Export:** Download your data in portable formats (JSON/CSV)
- **Delete:** Remove all collected data (via "Clear All Data" button)
- **Opt-out:** Uninstall the extension at any time

Since all data is stored locally and we do not collect or transmit any data externally, you have complete control over all of these rights directly within the extension.

---

**Summary:** fscrape stores all data locally on your device. No data is sent anywhere. You have complete control over your data at all times.

## Short Description (132 characters max)

Track Reddit posts effortlessly as you browse. Analyze engagement patterns, discover optimal posting times, and export your data.

## Detailed Description

**Track Reddit Posts Automatically While You Browse**

fscrape is a powerful Chrome extension that helps content creators, marketers, and Reddit enthusiasts understand engagement patterns on Reddit by automatically tracking posts as you scroll through subreddits.

**How It Works:**
1. 📌 Pin any subreddit you want to track
2. 📜 Browse Reddit normally - posts are automatically saved as you scroll
3. 📊 Analyze your data with interactive dashboards and heatmaps

**Key Features:**

🔥 **Engagement Heatmaps**
- Visualize post performance by day and hour
- Identify peak engagement times for any subreddit
- Filter by post count, average score, or comment activity

⏰ **Optimal Posting Times**
- Discover the best times to post for maximum engagement
- Get data-driven recommendations based on actual subreddit activity
- See performance ratings for each time slot

📊 **Interactive Dashboard**
- Posts over time trends
- Top subreddits by activity
- Most engaged posts rankings
- Comprehensive data filters

📥 **Export Your Data**
- Export to JSON or CSV format
- Compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and data analysis tools
- Take your data anywhere

🔒 **Privacy First**
- All data stored locally in your browser
- No data sent to external servers
- You own and control your data
- Works completely offline after installation

**Perfect For:**
- Content creators researching optimal posting strategies
- Social media managers tracking subreddit activity
- Market researchers analyzing Reddit trends
- Anyone interested in Reddit engagement patterns

**Getting Started:**
1. Install the extension
2. Visit any subreddit (e.g., reddit.com/r/programming)
3. Click the pin button that appears in the bottom-right corner
4. Scroll through posts normally - they'll be tracked automatically
5. Click the extension icon to view stats or open the dashboard

**Technical Details:**
- Works on all Reddit domains (www.reddit.com, old.reddit.com, sh.reddit.com)
- Lightweight and performant
- No Reddit API required
- Automatically handles Reddit's infinite scroll

Start tracking Reddit posts today and make data-driven decisions about when and where to post!
